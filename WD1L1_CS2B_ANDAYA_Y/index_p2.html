The Girl with Lavender Wings

A short story

        In a quiet village hidden within the mountains of Arcelia lived a girl named Airi. Unlike everyone else, she had beautiful lavender tinted wings hidden beneath her cloak.

        She kept them a secret, fearing that people would treat her differently. Everyday at dawn, she would quietly fly over the flower fields, soaring through the open skies. There, she felt truly free.

        One day, a strong storm struck the village. A young boy had been frolicking in the forest slipped near the edge of a cliff and was left hanging on for dear life. Without hesitation, Airi spread her wings and flew through the rain to save him. She carried him safely back to the village, ultimately revealing her secret to everyone.

        Realizing the situation, Airi immediately tried to fly away, afraid of what the villagers would say. Just as she spread her wings, a gentle hand stopped her. It was the boy's mother. She pulled Airi into a warm embrace, thanking her over and over again for saving her son's life.

"Thank you for saving my son."

        One by one, the villagers stepped forward, thanking and praising her for her bravery. For a moment, Airi couldn't speak, but then tears welled up in her eyes. She felt like a weight had been lifted from her chest. For the first time in years, she smiled and spread her wings without hiding.

        From that day on, Airi no longer hid her wings. She soared freely above the flower fields, no longer alone but surrounded by the people who accepted her for who she was.

End of Story
